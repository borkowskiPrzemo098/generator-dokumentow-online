# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/vanilla JS, no framework, hosted on GitHub Pages (repo `borkowskiPrzemo098/generator-dokumentow-online`). Shared chrome (header, footer, tiles, related links) is generated by `tools/build.mjs` from `assets/js/catalog.js`; the committed HTML is the deployed output.

## Users

Anyone arriving from Google who needs one concrete document right now ("wypowiedzenie umowy wzór", "generator CV", "upoważnienie do odbioru"). Mostly on a phone, often mid-task: quitting a gym, applying for a job, sending a formal complaint, printing a sign-in sheet. They want a correct, decent-looking PDF in minutes and leave.

## Product Purpose

A free Polish portal of 30 document generators (CV, formal letters, organisational sheets, business cards, occasion cards). Success: the visitor finds the right generator, fills a short form with a live preview, and downloads a clean A4 PDF without registering. Long term it is monetised with ads, so it must look like a credible, well-kept publication.

## Positioning

Everything happens in the browser: no account, no upload, the document updates while typing and prints to PDF directly. Sister site of **Kalkulatory Online** (same owner, same design family in a different lead colour).

## Operating Context

- Entry is almost always a generator page from search, not the homepage.
- Phone-first: on phones the form comes first and a fixed action bar offers "Podgląd" (full-screen preview) and "Pobierz PDF"; on desktop the form sits beside a sticky preview panel.
- PDF export uses the browser print dialog ("Zapisz jako PDF").
- Templates are general-purpose aids, not legal advice; every page says so.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- 30 generators in 6 categories: Kariera (6), Pisma formalne (11), Organizacja i biznes (6), Wizerunek firmy (2), Okolicznościowe (3), Zdrowie i sport (2).
- Colour/theme pickers on CV, business card, invitation and greeting card.
- Deliberately excluded: lease and loan agreements, VAT invoices (legal/tax risk).
- No backend, no paid APIs, no data leaves the device; localStorage only for cookie consent.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: **Generator dokumentów online** (binding).
- Visual family: sibling of Kalkulatory Online — same structure, type, tile and field language, sun-yellow accent — but its own lead colour (user decision 2026-09-26). Homepage tiles show icon + full name only.
- Popular generators first (6), the rest grouped into category tabs with a "show all" option (user request).

## Evidence on Hand

- Real content: the 30 working generators and their templates.
- Absent and must not be fabricated: user counts, ratings, testimonials, download statistics, press, partner logos.

## Product Principles

1. The document first: the live preview and the download action are always one tap away.
2. Credibility through craft and transparency (local processing, honest disclaimers), never through invented social proof.
3. Every generator page is a landing page; the homepage is secondary.
4. Adding the 31st generator must cost no design work: chrome and listings come from the catalog.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Labels on every field, visible focus states, keyboard operability, WCAG AA contrast, tap targets of at least 44px, readable text at 1rem or larger.
